My story

I am a doctoral researcher working at the intersection of technology, gender, and conflict studies. My work is grounded in a simple but urgent question: when political unrest disrupts everyday economic life, what role can digital tools realistically play in helping women sustain their livelihoods — and where do those tools fall short?

That question first took shape through fieldwork with women artisans practising Kashida embroidery in the Thar Desert of Western Rajasthan, where I explored how digital spaces were reshaping traditional craft economies. It has since grown into my doctoral thesis, which turns to my home state of Manipur and to Ima Keithel — the centuries-old, all-women-run market of Imphal — to study how vendors there adopt, adapt to, and are transformed by digital technology amid ongoing socio-political conflict.

I work with a mixed-methods approach: large-scale surveys to map patterns of digital access and use, paired with key informant interviews, focus group discussions, and case studies that surface the lived, human texture behind the numbers. I care about research that stays close to the people it studies — anonymised, ethically grounded, and useful beyond the thesis itself.

Academic journey

From coursework to fieldwork

2023

Admitted to the PhD programme

Joined the Department of Society Technology Interface, Central University of Rajasthan, as Research Scholar 2023PHDDS002, under the supervision of Dr. Wairokpam Premi Devi.

Jul 2024

Research Methodology Course, ICSSR

Completed the ICSSR-sponsored Research Methodology Course for Research Scholars in Social Sciences at the Council for Social Development, Hyderabad (2–12 July 2024), and visited Tilonia Barefoot College.

Oct 2024

First conference presentation

Presented "Uncovering the Journey of Women Artisans in Kashida Crafts from the Thar Desert of Western Rajasthan in the Digital Era" at a national seminar hosted by Shri Ratanlal Kanwarlal Patni Girl's College, Kishangarh.

Dec 2024

Coursework completed

Completed doctoral coursework classes and the NEP sensitisation course, alongside a literature review of 40+ articles and one thesis in the research area.

May 2025

International conference

Presented "Awareness of Digital Technologies: A Case Study on Women Vendors of Ima Market, Manipur" online at an international conference organised by Sage University, Indore.

Jan – Mar 2026

Quantitative fieldwork

Conducted survey fieldwork with 210 women vendors across Ima Keithel's three market complexes using stratified random sampling.

2026 – Present

Thesis in progress

Currently analysing survey and interview data for my doctoral thesis, Resilience and Digital Transformation of Women Vendors in Conflict-Prone Manipur.
